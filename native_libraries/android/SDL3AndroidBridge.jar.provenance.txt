    sdl3-native-tools/android/build-jar.sh

sdl3-native-tools/SDL/android-project/app/src/main/java/org/libsdl/app/
(SDLActivity, SDL, SDLSurface, SDLAudioManager, SDLControllerManager,

(same SDL commit): libSDL3.so's JNI_OnLoad registers the native methods these
classes declare, and both android-* gates check that all 66 are registered.
